# Fact check: `<Subject N>` vs `<Picture N>`, FL2VA vs Ref2VA, and the long-video papers (2026-11-07)

Checked 2026-11-07 20:15 EST against primary sources, by a Claude agent for Dylan (matlowai). The claims came from a ChatGPT brainstorm on "world state" for long video that Dylan pasted in. Quotes are short because the fetch tool would not return long verbatim passages; where a quote is the fetcher's paraphrase it is marked (paraphrase). Read-only; nothing was changed.

## Claim 1: `<Subject N>` vs `<Picture N>` in the official Ref2VA prompt guide: VERIFIED

| Item | Verdict | Source | Exact text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subject = reusable content | verified | [VIDEO_PROMPT_WRITING_GUIDE_ref_en.md](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/blob/main/docs/VIDEO_PROMPT_WRITING_GUIDE_ref_en.md) (Section 2.1) | `<Subject N>` is "a content unit that will actually be used in the target video, rather than the source file itself." Covers people, animals, objects, scenes, props, styles. One Subject can draw on several assets; one asset can supply several Subjects. |
| Picture = concrete frame anchor | verified | same, Section 2.2 | "A reference image used as a concrete target frame or shot-planning anchor." Standalone entry when the image "serves as a shot's first frame, keyframe, last frame, edited keyframe, or composition anchor." Also storyboard/shot-planning images (own line, naming the shots). |
| A Picture is cited INSIDE a Subject | verified | same, Sections 2.2 and 7 | "If an image is used only to define a character, scene, costume, or style, do not create a standalone picture entry." / "Instead, cite the image source inside the corresponding `<Subject N>` definition." Examples: "`<Subject 1>` is the young woman in `<Picture 1>`, with long dark hair, a blue cardigan, and a thin silver necklace." / "`<Subject 2>` is the fluffy white Samoyed in `<Picture 2>`, `<Picture 3>`, and `<Picture 4>`," (no standalone lines for Pictures 2-4). Also: "`<Subject 1>` is the woman whose appearance comes from `<Picture 1>` and whose walking motion comes from `<Video 1>`." |
| First frame | verified | same | A first frame is a standalone entry: "`<Picture 2>` is the first frame of [Shot 1]."; in the description: "the shot begins from `<Picture 1>`." |
| Where the tags live | verified | [MiniMax-H3 model card](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3); [skills](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3/tree/main/skills) (`h3-prompt-writing/references/ref-en.txt`); FL2VA/base guide `docs/VIDEO_PROMPT_WRITING_GUIDE_base_en.md` | Ref2VA prompts have a `subject_definitions` section first, then `summary` etc. Model card example: "`<Subject 1>` is the young man with short wavy blonde hair, wearing a bright pink suit jacket," and "`<Audio 2>` is the voice timbre reference for `<Subject 1>`'s voice". |

Core ComfyUI's MiniMax H3 node only knows the Picture/Video/Audio tags ("`<Picture i>` / `<Video k>` / `<Audio j>`"); it never emits Subject tags, so `<Subject N>` is purely prompt text the model reads.

**What it means for prompt writers.** Citing every reference as a standalone `<Picture N>` (which is what Dylan's tools did at the time, Cast pictures and set plates included) is a legal reading of the tags, but not the documented form. The guide reserves standalone `<Picture N>` lines for frames (first/last/keyframe, composition anchor, storyboard); identity and place references should be defined as `<Subject N>` with their picture cited inside the definition. Whether the model actually does better with the documented form is a separate question: it was measured afterwards (M7 in [prompt_craft_rules.md](prompt_craft_rules.md)): Cast as Subject was neutral, and wrapping a location master in a Subject did not stop takes cutting to it.

**What the tools load.** As of 2026-11-07, the public [ComfyUI-MAINodes](https://github.com/matlowai/ComfyUI-MAINodes) "Pika + reference pictures" preset (v1.2.4) used the FL2VA checkpoint and the FL2VA adapter under Ref2V conditioning: the wrong pair. Dylan's development branch uses the Ref2VA checkpoint with the Pika Ref2VA adapter; publishing that fix to the public main is pending. The extend (continuation) graph runs the FL2VA pair with no pictures, which is correct for a continuation from the last frames, but it means a Ref2V take is extended on a different model family than the take it continues, with its pictures dropped.

## What claims 1 and 2 mean for long video

1. **Prompts:** define identity and place references as `<Subject N>` with their picture cited inside; keep standalone `<Picture N>` for frames (opening frame, camera plate, storyboard/key). The two tag spaces count separately.
2. **Extend and rolling references:** FL2VA and Ref2VA are different checkpoints with different tendencies (the AMA calls Ref2VA quality a known weakness being worked on). An FL2VA extend carries no pictures, so rolling references need either a Ref2VA arm on the Ref2VA pair (checkpoint plus the Pika ref2va adapter) or the latent/first-frame route on FL2VA. The two must not be mixed in one comparison; an fl2va checkpoint + ref2va LoRA hybrid is off Pika's contract and is its own arm. This is how [u46h_rolling_refs.md](u46h_rolling_refs.md) is split.
